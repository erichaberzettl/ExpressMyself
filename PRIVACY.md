# ExpressMyself Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

ExpressMyself is a lightweight language-learning app and Chrome extension for browsing idioms, expressions, and everyday phrases.

## What data the extension stores

The Chrome extension stores only:

- your selected language
- the list of expressions you save for later review

This information is stored locally in your browser using Chrome extension storage.

## Website sync

So your saved phrases and language choice stay consistent between the extension
and the ExpressMyself website, the extension includes a small content script
that runs only on `https://expressmyself.vercel.app`. It copies your saved
phrase list and selected language between the extension's storage and the
website's local storage on your own device. It does not read page content,
track browsing, or send any of this data to a server. This is why the extension
requests access to `expressmyself.vercel.app`.

## What data the extension does not collect

ExpressMyself does not collect:

- account information
- names
- email addresses
- payment information
- browsing history
- page contents
- keystrokes
- location data

## Data sharing

ExpressMyself does not sell, transfer, or share personal data with third parties.

## Remote services

The extension does not require a backend service to function and does not send saved-expression data to a remote server.
